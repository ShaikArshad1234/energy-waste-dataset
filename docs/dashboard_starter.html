Plant Energy Dashboard

Synthetic telemetry for two plants over 90 days: where energy goes, when demand breaches the contract, and which machines waste power. Every anomaly shown was injected on purpose and labelled in the ground truth.

Daily energy by plant

Daily total from each plant's main incomer meter register, in kWh.

Peak demand against contract

Highest 15-minute demand of each local day on the main meter. Filled dots mark days above the contract demand.

Anomalies: labelled and detected

What the generator injected, against what the pipeline found from source data alone.

Counts use different units for some types. Idle and intensity findings are per machine-day or machine-week, while a label can cover a longer period. Hover a row for recall.

Idle waste by machine

Energy intensity drift

Each dot is one batch: its kWh per unit divided by the same machine and product in the first two weeks. Shaded band is the labelled drift period.

Recommendations

Generated from measured data only. Savings are per 30 days with a 90% capture factor.
